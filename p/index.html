About

Hello, my name is Büşra Aydoğan. I am 23, and I am a student at Yaşar University in İzmir.

I like to draw, explore new places and design new stuff. Drawing is how I think: a sketch comes before almost everything I make. Walking through a city I do not know teaches me how people find their way, and that feeds straight into how I plan navigation and layout.

Since 2021 I have also owned a coffee roasting business. Roasting taught me patience, small adjustments and listening to customers. A roast is never finished until someone tastes it, and I want my websites to work the same way: they answer the visitor.

In the future I want to be a web designer and a good communicator, someone who can talk with a client, understand what they need and turn it into a site that feels clear, friendly and a little surprising.
